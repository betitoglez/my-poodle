# Ilustraciones de prendas

Se usó ImageGen integrado con transparencia. Las tres llamadas usaron la prenda original de perfil como referencia de material y los sprites `front__hair_natural.webp`, `side__hair_natural.webp` y `back__hair_natural.webp` como referencias de ajuste. El recorte de cada ángulo preserva el alfa original.

## Texto de generación

```text
Create one landscape sprite sheet for the illustrated pet game. Subject: {subject}. The first reference gives identity and materials; the other three are body silhouettes for fit. Show THREE isolated garment-only sprites laid out in equal-width columns in ONE horizontal row: FRONT in the left third, RIGHT-FACING PROFILE in center third, REAR in right third. Each sprite must fit entirely within its own third with empty gutters, sprites large and centered in the cells. Show true orthographic views as the garment would fit this quadrupedal poodle, ghost mannequin technique. Remove mannequin completely, so only fabric and fasteners remain. Front and rear are viewed straight on and side shows its length. Accurately render cloth thickness, softly painted fabric, weave and soft top-left highlights, contact shadows within seams, like the reference. Clean deliberate openings only where the neck and paws emerge. Use clean outlines and opaque fabric with no pinholes. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no background/matte, no fur, paws, dog parts, tags, captions, cast shadows or text. The three views must be the same garment.
```

Isolated garment accessory only, left front orthographic, middle exact right-facing profile, right back orthographic, tailored to the poodle neck silhouettes in those reference views. {subject} Render as a carefully illustrated realistic game material with soft top-left light. Keep scale and material consistent. The rear wall must be hidden as it would be by the poodle: show the visible parts of the fabric only. Full genuine transparent RGBA silhouette, no dog, fur, model, ground shadows, text, captions, guides, or background. No points or triangular bib. Each accessory must be completely separated from its neighbors.
